Programming is the art and science of instructing computers to perform specific tasks through the use of structured instructions, known as code. It is a creative process that involves problem-solving and logical thinking, where programmers write algorithms and design solutions to address real-world problems. Programming languages serve as the communication bridge between humans and machines, allowing developers to express their ideas in a way that computers can understand and execute.
